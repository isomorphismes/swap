# MATH-LANG-A01: braid words are not endpoint permutations

Parent: https://github.com/isomorphisms/ai-ci/issues/244

This is an additive, headless language experiment. It does not replace Swap's
application, its raw-Idriç branch, its graphics contracts, or its current APK.

## Mathematical contract

A braid word has four signed generators on three strands. Words are read in
chronological order: the left word is performed before the right word. Endpoint
orders list the identities occupying the three successive slots. Each endpoint
type contains exactly the six bijective orders; duplicate identities cannot be
constructed. No particular byte layout is promised.

`BraidEquivalent` / `_≈_` is generated by reflexivity, symmetry, transitivity,
prefix congruence, inverse cancellation with any suffix, and the positive Artin
relation with any suffix. `append_context` / `append-context` establishes right
congruence. This is a setoid presentation of B3, not a quotient primitive, a
normal form algorithm, a braid-equality decision procedure, or a formal proof
of its equivalence with geometric isotopy. In particular, propositional equality
of the underlying words remains different from braid equivalence.

An action must supply actual proofs of inverse cancellation and the braid
relation. Induction on a braid-equivalence derivation establishes that every
such action respects braid equivalence. Composition is proved for arbitrary
words and every starting state, not only a sample list of braids.

Two concrete actions instantiate this interface: endpoint permutations and
signed exponent sum modulo 3. The latter separates a same-sign double crossing
from the empty word, even though their endpoint permutations are identical.
Consequently a function which reflects all endpoint equalities back into braid
equivalences is impossible. A single over-crossing and its under-crossing
inverse are also separated. These negative results concern braid equivalence,
not merely unequal source strings.

The six endpoint orders have explicit preimages, giving constructive
surjectivity. The finite winding invariant is deliberately incomplete. It must
not become an implementation of braid equality or a full winding count.

## Language result and limits

The first hypothesis is that explicit relation-preserving actions suffice;
no new syntax, quotient axiom or automatic proof search should be required.
Acceptance must be determined with the exact Idriç revision, not inferred from
Agda or from the similar source structure. The paired encodings share the
mathematical contract and fixtures; neither is an independent oracle for a
mistake in that contract.

A possible library improvement is a reusable relation-respecting observation
interface: legal quotient observations carry preservation laws, while equality
reflection needs separate evidence. This is not yet a demonstrated compiler
limitation. Do not open a language bug merely because an application omitted
an invariant.

All checking and runtime statuses are recorded in the parent checkpoint and
exact-head CI. Until a successful check is observed these are proof attempts,
not certified proofs. No Android lowering, frame submission, touch response,
physical display, general Bn, RSK, or geometric isotopy is certified here.
